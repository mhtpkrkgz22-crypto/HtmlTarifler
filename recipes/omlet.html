Omlet


Explanation

An omelet is an egg dish that can be described by its long, oval shape and smooth surface.

The inside typically has a relatively juicy and soft consistency. The outside should not be browned. It can be varied with omelets, vegetables, mushrooms, bacon, ragout, cheese, etc.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Recipe Steps

  1. For the omelet, place 4 eggs in a deep mixing bowl.
  2. Mix in half a teaspoon of salt and pepper. Using a whisk, beat well from bottom to top until air bubbles form.
  3. Preheat your pan and heat 1 tablespoon each of butter and olive oil.
  4. Next, pour the egg mixture into the pan. Turn the heat down low and cook until the bottom is browned.
  5. When the bottom of the omelet is browned and the top is still slightly runny, tilt your pan and fold the omelet in half.
  6. Cook for another 1-2 minutes, then remove from heat.
